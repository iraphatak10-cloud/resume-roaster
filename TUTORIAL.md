# How to use Roast / Office

1. Open the site and paste a resume, or upload a `.pdf` or `.txt` file.
2. Click Begin Analysis. The score, roast, and suggestions appear under Verdict.
3. Create an account if you want past reviews saved. Sign up needs a name, an email, and a password of at least 8 characters.
4. Sign in later to open those saved reviews.

PDF text is extracted on the server before the review runs. A scanned image with no text will be rejected. Paste the words instead.

The review is limited to 10,000 characters, and repeated requests from the same person are slowed down.
